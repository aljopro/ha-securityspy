- (change) PROBE RESULT - ASSUMPTION 1 RESOLVED. Live ++eventStream capture (100s, 1341 events) settles the classification design. CLASSIFY is NOT a discrete already-ended detection - it fires repeatedly per-frame during a motion episode (191 CLASSIFY on cam 10 in 95s, gaps 0-2s) and confidence is VOLATILE, swinging 4->100 within one person walking through frame (e.g. 20,69,19,77,88,8,54,25,5,18...97,96,99,99). Therefore: HUMAN/VEHICLE/ANIMAL must be BINARY SENSORS with hysteresis/debounce over a confidence threshold, NOT one-shot event entities. A naive event-per-CLASSIFY would fire ~190 events per person per 90s. Event entity should fire ONCE per episode on threshold crossing.
